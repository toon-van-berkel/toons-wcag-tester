# WCAG 2.2 — Success Criteria

Complete reference list for `toons-wcag-tester`, including all active Level A, AA and AAA success criteria. Level AAA conformance requires satisfying all applicable criteria at all three levels, as well as the WCAG conformance requirements.

| Level     | Criteria | Cumulative |
| --------- | -------: | ---------: |
| A         |       31 |         31 |
| AA        |       24 |         55 |
| AAA       |       31 |         86 |
| **Total** |   **86** |     **86** |

## Documentation

Each success criterion includes two official W3C references:

- **WCAG ID:** The normative requirements of the success criterion.
- **Understanding:** Detailed explanations, intent, benefits, examples, sufficient techniques, advisory techniques and common failures.

Understanding documents provide informative guidance and are not themselves normative requirements.

## Notes

- Criterion 4.1.1 (Parsing) was removed in WCAG 2.2 and is not included.
- Automated tests alone cannot establish full WCAG conformance.
- Understanding documents, techniques and ACT Rules provide implementation and evaluation guidance.
- Sufficient techniques are not the only valid methods of satisfying a success criterion.
- Individual test rules may cover only part of a success criterion.
- Manual or assisted evaluation may be required to establish full criterion coverage.
- This list is a reference index, not the full normative requirements or a claim of conformance.
